Split the squad into 5 training groups of 5, where players who are strong in a style work with those who are weak in it

03 · THE STYLE SPACE

Each player is a point z = Bᵀx in 3D

The roles separate on their own, although PCA never sees them.

b₁ · 39.5% of the varianceContact (rucks, tackles, lineouts) ↔ wide game (metres, defenders beaten)
b₂ · 29.7% of the varianceBall carrier (carries, offloads) ↔ low carriers: playmakers and props
b₃ · 10.5% of the varianceDominant tackles and passes ↔ carries and rucks

b₃ helps balance the groups but is less stable (λ₃ and λ₄ are close): work directions use only b₁ and b₂.

04 · SWAPS · ANSWER TO QUESTION 2

Every group should look like the whole squad

In the 3-component space SS_tot = SS_between + SS_within is fixed: minimising SS_between is the same as maximising the variety inside each group. At each step the swap of two players that lowers SS_between the most is applied, and the group means (X) move towards the centre.

05 · THE GROUPS

Every group covers the whole style space

Forwards and backs, contact and wide game in the same group; each group mean stays close to 0 on all three axes.

06 · WORK DIRECTIONS

Who to train, on what, with which mentor

PCA describes styles, not levels: each player is compared with the mean of their role. The mentor is the teammate in their group who is strongest on those statistics.

The other 20 players are at or above the mean of their role.
